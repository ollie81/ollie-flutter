Ollie – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This policy explains what Ollie collects, why, and how you can control it. Ollie is an AI companion app — it uses AI models to hold conversations, remember things you tell it, and reply with a real voice when you use voice features.

Information we collect

  • Account info: your phone number, a hashed password (we never store your actual password), and optionally your date of birth (used only to confirm you meet the minimum age) and country.
  • Conversations: the messages you send Ollie, Ollie's replies, and things Ollie infers from those conversations to remember you better — interests, mood patterns, goals you mention, and events you ask to be reminded about.
  • Voice: if you use voice features, your recording is sent to our transcription and text-to-speech providers to process your message and generate Ollie's spoken reply. We don't keep a copy of the audio itself once it's processed.
  • Usage & subscription info: how many messages you've sent, whether you have an active subscription, and — if you subscribe — the purchase information Google Play gives us to confirm and manage that subscription.
  • Device info: a push notification token, so we can send you notifications you've opted into.

Automatic safety checks

  • Messages are automatically screened for signs of self-harm, abuse, or other crisis situations, and for content that violates our usage policies. This happens on every message, whether or not anything is flagged.
  • If a message is flagged, that's recorded (the message and a timestamp) so it can be reviewed. Flagging never blocks your conversation — Ollie still replies normally, and, for crisis-related flags, includes a resource line alongside its normal reply.

How we use this information

  • To run the core app: hold a conversation, remember context between sessions, and generate voice replies.
  • To personalize Ollie's responses based on what you've told it.
  • To enforce free-tier limits and manage premium subscriptions.
  • To monitor for safety issues as described above.
  • To send you push notifications you've opted into.

Third parties we share data with

  • OpenAI — processes your messages to generate Ollie's replies, transcribes voice messages, and runs the automatic safety screening.
  • Papla Media — converts Ollie's replies to spoken audio for voice features.
  • Twilio — sends the one-time codes used to verify your phone number.
  • SendGrid — sends the one-time codes used to verify your email address, if you sign up or log in with email instead of phone.
  • Google — Play Billing (subscription purchases), Sign-In (if you use it to log in), Firebase (push notifications), and AdMob (the optional rewarded ads that unlock bonus messages).
  • Supabase and Railway — host our database and backend infrastructure.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't share it for third-party advertising.

Your choices

  • Clear Ollie's memory of you at any time from Settings — this erases what Ollie has learned about you (interests, patterns, things you've shared).
  • Export a copy of your data at any time from Settings.
  • Delete your account from Settings — this permanently removes your account and everything associated with it after a short grace period.
  • Turn push notifications on or off from Settings.

Children's privacy

Ollie is not intended for children under 13, and we ask for a birthdate at signup to help enforce that minimum.

Security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. Access tokens are stored securely on your device. We use industry-standard practices to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll let you know in the app.
